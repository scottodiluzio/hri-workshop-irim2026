About the workshop

Bridging intelligent robotics research and real-world deployment

Human-Robot Interaction is becoming a defining challenge for the next generation of intelligent robotic systems. As robots move into healthcare, rehabilitation, public spaces, homes and collaborative workplaces, they must interact naturally, safely and effectively with people.

This workshop places HRI at its core while focusing on the software infrastructures that enable the transition from research prototypes to real-world robotic applications. Particular attention is given to modular, reproducible and maintainable architectures, with ROS 2 as an enabling middleware for interoperability across platforms and application domains.

The workshop also highlights experiences from national and European collaborative research projects, including Horizon Europe initiatives, where shared software frameworks and open-source practices can accelerate technology transfer and collaboration.

Research themes

Software architectures & middleware

Design, integration and deployment of intelligent Human-Robot Interaction systems.

ROS 2 for intelligent robotics

Interoperability, modularity, maintainability and reproducibility across robotic platforms.

03

Multimodal interaction

Vision, speech, language, gesture, haptics, multimodal sensing and human intention recognition.

04

AI for HRI

Generative AI, foundation models, embodied AI, cognitive reasoning and adaptive robot behavior.

05

Planning & decision making

Cognitive architectures for interactive, collaborative and autonomous robotic systems.

06

Human-centred applications

Healthcare, rehabilitation, assistive robotics, service robotics and collaborative industry.

07

Open-source & reproducibility

Software ecosystems, benchmarking, interoperability and best practices for robotics engineering.

08

From lab to deployment

Technology transfer, collaborative projects and scalable, trustworthy real-world HRI systems.

Expected outcomes

Building collaborations beyond I-RIM 2026

The workshop aims to strengthen connections across the robotics community and ongoing national and European initiatives in Human-Robot Interaction and intelligent robotic systems.

Presentation materials, subject to speakers' permission, will be made available through this website. The organizers also plan to build on the workshop discussions toward a Special Session at IEEE/RSJ IROS 2027 in Florence, Italy.
